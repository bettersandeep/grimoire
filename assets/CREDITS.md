# Credits

The Grimoire icon and banner use "The Enchanter Merlin" by Howard Pyle, from *The Story of King Arthur and His Knights* (1903).
The drawing is in the public domain.
Source: [Wikimedia Commons, Arthur-Pyle The Enchanter Merlin.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arthur-Pyle_The_Enchanter_Merlin.JPG).

The banner, icon, benchmark card and screenshots are built from the HTML files in `src/` with headless Chrome.
Fonts: Geist Serif, Geist and Geist Mono by Vercel (SIL Open Font License), loaded from the `geist` npm package at render time.
Screenshots show real Grimoire output on NYC taxi trip data (NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission).
